Chord mode reads your keys directly, so turn Plover off first. You see exactly which keys you held on both keyboards, and the chord is checked when you let go of every key.

Plover mode is the real thing. Turn Plover on, click the box, and stroke the word. Plover types it, and the page checks the word it typed.
